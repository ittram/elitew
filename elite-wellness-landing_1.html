Inside

Free weights, resistance machines and cardio — treadmills and indoor bikes — plus a studio for classes. The floor was refitted in 2022, and full-height glass doors keep it bright with the air moving.

Outside

A functional training deck and bikes in the garden. When the weather's good, training moves outdoors — that's how this gym started, and it never stopped.
