A functional training deck and bikes in the garden. When the weather's good, training moves outdoors. That's how this gym started, and it never stopped.

Good to know

Can I train without a membership?

Yes. A day pass is €10, a week is €29. Walk in and pay at the desk.

Do I need to book?

No. Just come by during opening hours.

Do you speak English?

Yes, English and Portuguese.

Are there showers and lockers?

[PLACEHOLDER: confirm and answer. Example: "Yes, changing rooms with showers and lockers. Bring your own padlock."]

Do you provide towels?

No. Bring your own towel and a water bottle.

Is there air conditioning?

No. The full-height glass doors open onto the outdoor deck, so the air moves. It's how the gym was designed.

Do you have a squat rack?

Smith machines and a full free-weight setup, but no power racks. [PLACEHOLDER: confirm after the remodel. Example: "We added a half rack in 2026."]

Are you open on public holidays?

No, we close on Portuguese national holidays and on 27 October, Lagos's municipal holiday. Our Google profile always shows the current hours.

Do you do personal training?

Yes, one-to-one with a qualified coach. Ask at the desk or message us for prices.
