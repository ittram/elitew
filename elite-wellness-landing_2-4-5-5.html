    Good to know

    Can I train without a membership?

    Yes. A day pass is €10 and a week pass is €35.90, insurance included. Walk in and pay at the desk.

    Is there a minimum commitment?

    Not for visitor passes. Member plans are paid every 2 weeks, from €13, plus the €24 sports insurance once a year. todo Ask us on WhatsApp.

    Do I need to book?

    No. Just come by during opening hours.

    Do you speak English?

    Yes, English and Portuguese.

    Are there showers and lockers?

    Yes. Showers and lockers in both changing rooms. The lockers need your own lock; if you don't have one, you can buy one at reception.

    Do you provide towels?

    No. Bring your own towel, water and trainers. If you forget your towel, you can buy one at reception.

    Is there air conditioning?

    No. The full height glass doors open onto the outdoor deck, so the air moves. It's how the gym was designed.

    Do you have a squat rack?

    Yes. Two squat racks, Smith machines and a full free weight setup.

    Are you open on public holidays?

    No. We're closed on all Portuguese public holidays and on Lagos's municipal holiday (27 October).

    Do you do personal training?

    Yes, one to one with a qualified coach. Ask us on WhatsApp for prices.
